Open Agentic Diorama Market

Three agentic websites that find each other through their published agent-manifest.json and negotiate in your browser over window.postMessage. Bring your own model with Mozilla Otari, run Gemini Nano on-device, or use Demo Mode with no key.

OpenAgentic Procurement HubType a commission; agents write the RFQ, discover the studio, negotiate within your budget and ask you to approve the order. Miniature WorldsArtisan studio: fast and premium. Live Three.js designer, quotes, a 12% negotiation floor and a commission board. Frostbite MiniaturesWorkshop for dense natural detail: cheaper, slower, an 8% floor. Same open runtime, its own agents and prices.

Start with the Hub and click Run commission: both studios design their own 3D diorama side by side, you compare the quotes, choose one, and the hub negotiates and asks you to approve the order.
